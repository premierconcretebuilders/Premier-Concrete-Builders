About Us

Located at 4551 145th Court, Basehor, Kansas 66007, Premier Concrete Builders is a dedicated local concrete contractor committed to structural integrity, clean job sites, and long-lasting craftsmanship.

Whether upgrading your home's curb appeal with a brand-new concrete driveway and backyard patio, safely demolishing cracked old slabs, or pouring reinforced commercial warehouse floors, our crew handles every phase—from excavation and proper sub-base compaction to precision pouring, reinforcement, and clean finishing.

Our Professional Concrete Services

Concrete Driveways

Boost your property's value and curb appeal with a heavy-duty, reinforced concrete driveway engineered to withstand Kansas freeze-thaw cycles and daily vehicle loads without cracking or sinking.

Concrete Patios

Transform your backyard into an inviting entertainment space with a custom-designed concrete patio. Choose from clean broom finishes, smooth trowel edges, or decorative borders tailored to your yard.

Sidewalks & Walkways

We pour safe, level, ADA-compliant concrete sidewalks and residential garden walkways with properly spaced control joints and slip-resistant finishes for lasting pedestrian safety.

Concrete Demolition

Need old, heaving, or broken concrete removed? Our crew safely breaks up, hauls away, and disposes of deteriorated driveways, slabs, patios, and foundations—leaving your site graded and ready for new work.

Concrete Steps

Upgrade crumbling entryways with solid, precision-formed concrete steps and front porch stoops built with uniform riser heights, deep footings, and clean bullnose edges.

Warehouse Concrete

High-load, laser-screeded industrial warehouse floors, loading docks, and commercial shop slabs engineered with steel reinforcement and high-PSI mixes to support forklifts and heavy machinery.
